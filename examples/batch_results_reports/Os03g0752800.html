Function Summary:
The rice gene Os03g0752800, also known by its aliases OsMADS15 and OsMADS14, is a MADS-box transcription factor that plays a crucial role in the reproductive development and overall architecture of the rice plant. As an ortholog of the *Arabidopsis* gene *APETALA1* (AP1), it functions as an A-class gene in the ABCDE model of flower development, where it is essential for determining the identity of the palea and lodicules. Overexpression of OsMADS15 leads to significant phenotypic changes, including early flowering, reduced plant height, and alterations in root development and tiller formation. A key regulatory mechanism involves the Nhd1-OsMYB110-OsMADS15 module, which integrates nutrient availability with developmental timing by modulating flowering in response to phosphorus levels. While some MADS-box genes are implicated in abiotic stress responses, there is currently no direct evidence to suggest a primary role for Os03g0752800 in drought, cold, or salt tolerance, though indirect associations, such as its upregulation by a drought-tolerance-improving gene, have been noted.

Keywords:
MADS-box, flowering time, plant architecture, floral development, phosphorus sensing
