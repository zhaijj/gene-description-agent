Function Summary:



The *Zea mays* gene Zm00001eb126570, also known by the aliases DSD1 (decreased stomatal density 1) and ZmICEb, is a basic helix-loop-helix (bHLH) transcription factor that plays a pivotal role in stomatal development and abiotic stress tolerance. This gene functions by controlling critical stages of stomatal formation, including the differentiation of guard mother cells, the formation of subsidiary cells, and the maturation of guard cells. Specifically, mutations in DSD1/ZmICEb can cause an arrest in stomatal development, leading to a significant reduction in stomatal density on the leaf epidermis. Phenotypically, this decrease in stomata results in enhanced drought tolerance and improved water-use efficiency (WUE), mitigating yield loss under water-deficient conditions. The function of this gene is conserved across plant species, with its ortholog in *Arabidopsis thaliana* being ICE1 (INDUCER OF CBF EXPRESSION 1) and in rice, OsICE1/OsbHLH002.
